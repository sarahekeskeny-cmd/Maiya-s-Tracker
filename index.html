Each stage counts everyone who reached at least that point — so a client who's since moved to Waiting for Medical or In Force still counts toward "Said Yes" and "Applications submitted" too. "Approved as Other" is a separate outcome and isn't part of this funnel. Type a number in any "Override rate %" to swap in your own assumption — leave it blank to use the auto ratio. "Projected placed" chains both stages, so it estimates how much of today's Said Yes pipeline should ultimately go In Force.
